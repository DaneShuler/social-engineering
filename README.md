Social Engineering Awareness Presentation

Overview

This presentation educates audiences on the tactics, psychology, and prevention of social engineering attacks. It highlights how attackers manipulate human behavior to gain unauthorized access to sensitive systems or data and provides actionable defense strategies for individuals and organizations.

Objectives

Explain the concept and impact of social engineering.

Identify common attack types such as phishing, baiting, and pretexting.

Demonstrate how social engineers exploit trust and authority.

Present effective countermeasures to recognize and prevent attacks.

Key Topics

What Is Social Engineering?
Overview of manipulation-based cyber threats.

Attack Lifecycle:
Reconnaissance → Engagement → Exploitation → Exit.

Common Techniques:
Phishing, vishing, smishing, tailgating, and impersonation.

Real-World Examples:
Case studies of well-known social engineering incidents.

Defense Strategies:
Awareness training, multi-factor authentication, and verification protocols.

Learning Outcome

Viewers will gain the ability to identify social engineering attempts, understand attacker motivations, and apply best practices to protect personal and organizational data.
